Version: 1.0
Status: Living Reference Document
Target: Marketing Website, Council Revenue Platform & Product Documentation
________________________________________
1. Purpose
This document defines the official terminology used across the Kahyrah ecosystem.
Its purpose is to ensure that:
•	product terminology remains consistent; 
•	English and French versions communicate the same business concepts; 
•	marketing content reads naturally in each language; 
•	the operational application uses consistent labels; 
•	future translations remain coherent. 
This document is the authoritative source for all user-facing terminology.
________________________________________
2. Translation Principles
Meaning before literal translation
Translations must preserve business meaning rather than individual words.
Example
❌ Literal
Revenue Collection Platform
↓

Plateforme de collecte des recettes
Preferred
Council Revenue Management Platform
↓

Plateforme de gestion des recettes des collectivités
The second better reflects Kahyrah's operational scope.
________________________________________
Adapt to the target audience
The English website targets UK local authorities.
The French website targets French public-sector organisations.
Terminology should reflect the audience rather than being translated word for word.
________________________________________
Product-first language
Marketing should emphasise operational outcomes rather than technical implementation.
Avoid exposing internal technology names unless specifically relevant.
Example
❌
Prometheus metrics
Preferred
Operational Business Reporting

12. UI Translation Rules
•	Backend enums must never be translated. 
•	Database values remain language-neutral. 
•	APIs remain language-neutral. 
•	Only display labels are translated. 
Example:
Backend	English	French
PRESENCE_CONFIRMED	Presence Confirmed	Présence confirmée

13. Governance
This document is the authoritative reference for all Kahyrah terminology.
New terminology should follow these rules:
•	English is the source language. 
•	Every new English term must have an approved French equivalent before release. 
•	Marketing, documentation and application labels must all use the approved terminology. 
•	Literal translations should be avoided where a more natural public-sector equivalent exists. 
•	Changes to terminology should be reviewed centrally to maintain consistency across the entire Kahyrah ecosystem. 

14. Terminology Localisation Strategy
•	English terminology is the canonical product language.
•	French terminology should be adapted to the target public-sector organisation rather than translated literally.
•	For France, Kahyrah uses the following hierarchy:
Target	Preferred terminology
Generic marketing	Collectivités territoriales
Municipal-specific content	Communes
Local public administration	Administrations locales
•	This approach ensures that the marketing material remains inclusive of all potential French public-sector customers while using terminology that feels natural and familiar to decision-makers.
