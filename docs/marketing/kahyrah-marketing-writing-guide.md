# Kahyrah Marketing Writing Guide

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Marketing and localisation standard  
**Applies to:** Kahyrah marketing website, future marketing pages, brochures, proposals and product marketing copy

## 1. Purpose

This guide defines how Kahyrah and its products are described in English and French. It complements the detailed terminology and translation guide and provides the writing rules needed to keep future marketing copy consistent.

## 2. Brand architecture

### Kahyrah

Kahyrah is the **company**.

Approved concise company description:

**English**  
Kahyrah is a software company designing innovative solutions that simplify operational and financial processes across public- and private-sector organisations.

**French**  
Kahyrah est une entreprise technologique qui conçoit des solutions innovantes destinées à simplifier les processus opérationnels et financiers des organisations publiques et privées.

### Council Revenue Platform

Council Revenue Platform is a **product developed by Kahyrah**. It is currently Kahyrah's flagship operational platform for council revenue management.

Do not describe Kahyrah itself as:
- a revenue platform;
- a collection platform;
- an operational platform;
- the Council Revenue Platform.

Correct relationship examples:
- Kahyrah develops innovative software solutions.
- The Council Revenue Platform helps local authorities manage daily revenue operations.
- The Council Revenue Platform is Kahyrah's flagship operational platform.

## 3. Product naming

- Product names are proper names and remain untranslated.
- Use **Council Revenue Platform** in both English and French.
- Do not silently replace the product name with a descriptive French translation.
- Generic references such as *the platform / la plateforme* are acceptable after the product has been clearly introduced.
- Future product names should follow the same rule unless an explicit localisation decision is approved.

## 4. Marketing voice

Copy should be:
- professional;
- enterprise-oriented;
- operational;
- credible;
- concise;
- outcome-focused;
- understandable to non-technical decision-makers.

Avoid:
- exaggerated claims;
- startup hype;
- unnecessary technical jargon;
- architecture terminology where a business outcome is clearer;
- claims not supported by the product;
- describing a pilot as a demo.

## 5. Audience

Current marketing must work for:
- UK councils and local authorities;
- French collectivités territoriales and administrations locales;
- field operations teams;
- revenue teams and supervisors;
- public-sector decision-makers and partners.

Kahyrah as a company is broader than Civic Tech and may serve both public- and private-sector organisations. Do not define the company solely by the market of its first product.

## 6. Translation philosophy

French copy is an adaptation of the approved business meaning, not a word-for-word translation.

Rules:
1. Preserve the business intent.
2. Use natural French public-sector terminology.
3. Keep product names untranslated.
4. Prefer concise, idiomatic French.
5. Avoid English syntax transplanted into French.
6. Keep the same level of confidence in both languages.
7. Do not add partnership, service or product claims that are absent from the approved source meaning.

## 8. CTA standards

Preferred:
- **Request a Pilot** / **Demander un pilote**
- **Explore the Council Revenue Platform** / **Découvrir la Council Revenue Platform**
- **Explore the platform** / **Découvrir la plateforme**
- **Contact Us** / **Nous contacter**

Do not substitute:
- Book a Demo;
- Request a Demo;
- Démonstration pilote;

unless the commercial offer itself changes.

## 9. Pilot positioning

A Kahyrah pilot is a structured operational evaluation in the organisation's real operating context.

Marketing copy should convey:
- controlled operational scope;
- selected users/merchants/areas;
- structured onboarding;
- field-team use;
- measurable success criteria;
- operational monitoring.

Do not frame it as simply trying the software.

## 10. Product evidence

When screenshots or product visuals are shown:
- call them real operational screens when they are real;
- describe the business capability demonstrated, not merely the UI widget;
- do not invent product functions to make marketing copy stronger;
- keep screenshots and capability descriptions aligned.

## 11. Technical terminology

The marketing website should normally describe outcomes rather than implementation technology.

Prefer:
- operational dashboards;
- real-time visibility;
- complete traceability;
- payment recovery monitoring.

Avoid in general marketing copy unless the page is explicitly technical:
- Prometheus;
- Grafana;
- Kafka;
- Spring Boot;
- observability stack terminology.

## 12. Company versus product test

Before approving any sentence, ask:

**Is the subject Kahyrah or the Council Revenue Platform?**

If the sentence says what the software does, the subject should normally be the Council Revenue Platform or *the platform*.

If the sentence says what the organisation designs, develops, supports or stands for, the subject may be Kahyrah.

## 13. Cross-site consistency

Every page review must verify:
- company/product distinction;
- product-name preservation;
- approved terminology;
- CTA consistency;
- pilot terminology;
- natural French;
- consistent UK English;
- no unsupported capability claims.

## 14. Source-of-truth hierarchy

For implementation, use the following precedence:

1. Latest approved page translation/copy review.
2. Kahyrah Terminology & Translation Guide.
3. This Marketing Writing Guide.
4. Existing implementation copy.

When documents conflict, the more recent explicitly approved copy decision takes precedence and the standards should then be updated if the change is intended to be permanent.
