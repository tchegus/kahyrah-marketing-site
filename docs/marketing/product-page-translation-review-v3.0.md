# Kahyrah Product Page Translation Review

**Version 3.0 — Consolidated Product Page Baseline**

> This document supersedes Version 2.0 for the Product page. It consolidates the approved bilingual copy with the structural decisions resulting from the Home/Product capability audit: a business-operation-led Hero, relocation of the Operational Dashboard into the numbered capabilities, improved Revenue Reconciliation visual guidance, and renumbering of Audit Trail.

## Editorial and Brand Rules

- **Kahyrah** = company → designs and develops software solutions.
- **Council Revenue Platform** = product → manages council revenue operations.
- The Product Hero explains the **real-world council operation/problem** first; the real product screens below demonstrate **how the Council Revenue Platform solves it**.
- Never introduce capabilities that are not implemented or evidenced by the product.
- **Council Revenue Platform** remains untranslated in both English and French.
- French capability descriptions retain the established **infinitive convention** where applicable.
- Real product screenshots remain the primary evidence for implemented capabilities.
- OBR remains explicitly marked **IN DEVELOPMENT / EN COURS DE DÉVELOPPEMENT** until the broader reporting suite is delivered.

### v3.0 visual note

The approved Revenue Reconciliation copy remains unchanged. However, the website visual should be revisited because the previous crop does not show the full implemented reconciliation capability. Prefer a real screenshot/crop that exposes the broader reconciliation view, including the available expected, collected/recorded, supervisor, gap, area/status and action context where visible in the application. Do not compensate for a limited screenshot by adding unsupported marketing claims.

## Capability 07 --- Operational Dashboard

**Purpose:** Bring the operational capabilities together into one consolidated view after the visitor has understood the underlying daily workflow.

**Positioning:** Relocated from the former standalone **Product Preview / Operational Dashboard** section. It appears after Revenue Reconciliation and before Audit Trail.

**Design direction:** Present this as a wider “convergence” section rather than a normal alternating capability block. Use the real Operational Dashboard screenshot at a larger width so the relationship between revenue, collections, presence, recovery and alerts is immediately visible.
