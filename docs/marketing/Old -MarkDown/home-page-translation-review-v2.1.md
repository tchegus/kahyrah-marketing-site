**Scope:** Kahyrah marketing website — Home page

# Purpose

This document is the implementation reference for the approved English and French Home page copy. It deliberately contains only final approved wording; superseded wording and review commentary have been removed.

# Brand and terminology rules

- **Kahyrah** is the company.
- **Council Revenue Platform** is a product developed by Kahyrah. The product name is not translated.
- Company and product must never be presented as the same entity.
- French copy should express the approved business meaning naturally rather than translate English word-for-word.
- The marketing tone is professional, enterprise-oriented, operational, clear and credible.
- A **pilot** is a real structured operational pilot, not a software demo.
